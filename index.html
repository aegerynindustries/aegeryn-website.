04 / Core research directions

Designed to
adapt.

The AEGIS concept centers on three connected challenges: sensing the environment, coordinating multiple agents, and recovering when systems encounter failure.

01 / Perception

See what matters.

Research visual and thermal sensing for potential search targets, with confidence indicators and human review.

02 / Coordination

Move as one.

Develop distributed search planning and task assignment so cooperating agents can cover ground efficiently.

03 / Resilience

Keep going.

Model coordinator failover, degraded connectivity, and the safe reassignment of work when an agent goes offline.
