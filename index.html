    04 / Core research directions

    Designed to
    adapt.

    The AEGIS concept centers on three connected challenges: sensing the environment, coordinating multiple agents, and recovering when systems encounter failure.

    01 / Perception

    See what matters.

    Research visual and thermal sensing for potential search targets, with confidence indicators and human review.

    02 / Coordination

    Move as one.

    Develop distributed search planning and task assignment so cooperating agents can cover ground efficiently.

    03 / Resilience

    Keep going.

    Model coordinator failover, degraded connectivity, and the safe reassignment of work when an agent goes offline.

    05 / Development path

    Progress is
    engineered.

    We're starting with software and measurable simulation milestones. Any real-world drone work would follow later, with appropriate safety testing, permissions, and human supervision.

    01

    2D mission simulation

    Virtual search agents, area coverage, task assignment, and basic failures.

    Current focus
    02

    Multi-agent autonomy

    Distributed coordination, communication faults, and adaptive route planning.

    Planned
    03

    AI perception

    Visual and thermal detection of possible missing people in simulation, with confidence scores and human verification.

    Planned
    04

    Integration with flight simulation, environmental conditions, and sensor models.

    Future stage
    05

    Physical prototyping

    Controlled prototype validation after software, safety, and compliance work.
